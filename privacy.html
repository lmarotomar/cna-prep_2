Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CNA ProMaster ("we," "our," or "the product"), operated by MarDigital Hub, collects, uses, and protects information provided by users ("you") when purchasing or accessing our digital products.

1. Information We Collect

When you purchase a CNA ProMaster product, the following information may be collected:

  • Email address — provided at checkout for order confirmation and product delivery.
  • Payment information — processed securely by Stripe. We do not store your card details.
  • Purchase history — maintained by Beacons for order management purposes.
  • Usage data — anonymous analytics may be collected to improve our products.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used exclusively to:

  • Process and confirm your purchase.
  • Deliver the digital product(s) you purchased.
  • Respond to customer service inquiries.
  • Send important product updates (you may opt out at any time).

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Third-Party Services

CNA ProMaster uses the following third-party services:

  • Stripe — payment processing.
  • Beacons — digital product delivery and storefront.
  • GitHub Pages — hosting of the companion practice application.

4. Data Security

Payment transactions are encrypted using SSL technology and processed by Stripe, a PCI-DSS compliant provider. We do not store credit card or payment information on our servers.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data, and to opt out of any marketing communications at any time.

6. Cookies

Our digital products and the companion app do not use tracking cookies. Third-party platforms (Beacons, Stripe) may use cookies as part of their standard operations.

7. Children's Privacy

CNA ProMaster products are intended for adults pursuing professional certification. We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under the age of 18.

8. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy at any time. Continued use of our products after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
